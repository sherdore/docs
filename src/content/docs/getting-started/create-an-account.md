title: Create an account
description: Sign up for SherDore once and use the same sign-in for every product.
---

Every SherDore product uses one account, managed at **accounts.sherdore.com**. You sign up once, and the same login works across all products. Nothing to create or remember per product.

## Sign up

1. Go to [accounts.sherdore.com](https://accounts.sherdore.com) and choose **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password.
3. Open the verification email we send you and click the link.

Until your email is verified you can't use the products, so check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive. If it still isn't there after a few minutes, use **Resend** on the verification page, or contact support and we can verify the address for you.

## Name your organization

Your subscriptions belong to an **organization**, not to you personally. The first time you pick a product, you'll be asked to name your organization, which can be your business name. See [Your team](/getting-started/team-and-organizations/) for how organizations and members work.

## Start a product

1. From **Get started**, pick a product and its entry plan.
2. Choose **Start free trial**. No card is needed.
3. Use **Launch** to open the product. You arrive signed in, with no second login.

## How sign-in works across products

Each product checks with your SherDore account every time you open it. If your trial ends, a payment lapses or your account is suspended, access to the products stops, and it comes back as soon as the subscription is active again.

## Forgot your password?

Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and we'll email you a reset link. If you can't receive that email, contact support and an administrator can set a new password for you.
