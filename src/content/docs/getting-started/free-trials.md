---
title: Free trials
description: How the SherDore free trial works, and what happens when it ends.
---

SherDore has no free-forever plans. Instead, every product offers a **free trial of its entry plan** (usually called Starter), so you can try the real product before you pay.

## The rules

- **No card needed.** You can start a trial without entering any payment details.
- **Entry plan only.** The trial is on each product's entry plan. Higher plans don't have a trial, but you can upgrade once you're subscribed.
- **Once per product.** Each organization gets one trial per product. If you've already had a subscription to a product, in any state, you subscribe to continue.
- **Length.** Trials are 14 days unless the plan page says otherwise.

## Before it ends

We email the organization owner **3 days before** a trial ends, with a link to choose a plan. You can subscribe at any time during the trial, and you keep the days you have left.

## When it ends

If you haven't subscribed, access to that product stops when the trial ends. Your organization, team and account are untouched, and you can subscribe at any later time to carry on.

## Subscribing during or after a trial

Open **Get started** (or your organization page), pick the product and choose a payment method. See [Plans and billing](/getting-started/plans-and-billing/) for the options.
