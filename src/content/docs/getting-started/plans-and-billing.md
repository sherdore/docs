---
title: Plans and billing
description: Payment methods, renewals, changing plans, receipts and cancelling.
---

You manage everything about billing in one place, **Billing** in your SherDore account, for every product.

## Payment methods

You pick a payment method when you subscribe. Which ones are offered depends on what's switched on at the time:

| Method | How it renews |
|---|---|
| **Card** | Renews automatically each period. |
| **PayPal** | One payment buys one period. You pay again when it ends. |
| **Crypto** | One payment buys one period. You pay again when it ends. |
| **Bitcoin** | One payment buys one period. You pay again when it ends. |

For the pay-per-period methods nothing renews on its own. We email you before the period ends so you can pay again and keep your access.

## Changing plan

Open **Billing**, choose the subscription and pick a new plan. A change you schedule takes effect at the next billing period, and you can cancel a scheduled change before then.

## Cancelling

Cancel from **Billing**. Your access continues until the end of the period you've paid for. If you change your mind before it ends, use **Resume**.

## If a payment fails

When a card payment fails, the subscription becomes **past due**. You keep access for a **7-day grace period** so you can update your card. After that, access stops until payment succeeds.

## Receipts

Every payment has a receipt you can open from **Billing**.

## Prices

Each product page lists its current plans. The plan page in your account is always the final word on price.
