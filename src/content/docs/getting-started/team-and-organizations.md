## Organizations

An **organization** is the business or team that holds your subscriptions. A product subscription belongs to the organization, so everyone in it shares the same plan. You can belong to more than one organization and **switch** between them from your account.

## Roles in your account

| Role | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Created the organization. Manages billing and members. |
| **Admin** | Helps manage members and subscriptions. |
| **Member** | Uses the products. |

## Adding people

On your organization page, add a teammate by email. If they already have a SherDore account they join straight away. Otherwise they get an invitation by email. You can cancel an invitation that's still pending, and remove a member at any time. The owner can't be removed.

## Roles inside a product

Each product has its own permissions on top of your account role. When someone opens a product for the first time, their account role decides where they start. In [Uptime](/products/uptime/team-and-roles/), for example, an account owner starts as Owner, an admin as Admin, and a member as read-only. After that, each product's own member management applies.
