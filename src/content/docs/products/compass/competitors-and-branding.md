## Saved competitors

Keep a list of the rivals you track under **Competitors**, so you don't retype them for every analysis. Your plan sets how many you can save: 3 on Starter, 10 on Growth, 50 on Agency.

Compass finds each competitor's social profiles automatically when it runs, and you can add them by hand too. It recognises Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Yelp and Pinterest. You can export your competitor list as a CSV.

## Branding (white-label)

On **Growth** and **Agency**, open **Branding** to put your own logo and brand name on your reports. It's useful when you send reports to your own clients.
