SherDore Compass scores your site against your competitors' sites on performance, security, SEO, content and Google Business Profile, then gives you a ranked list of what to fix.

Open it at [compass.sherdore.com](https://compass.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## How it works

1. **Enter your domain.** Add your own site and, if you like, a label for the analysis.
2. **Pick your rivals.** Choose competitors from your saved list or type in new ones.
3. **Compass audits them all.** It crawls each site, runs Google PageSpeed Insights and checks Google Business Profile details, then scores every dimension.
4. **Read it, share it, act on it.** Open the side-by-side report, send the link, and work through the ranked fixes.
