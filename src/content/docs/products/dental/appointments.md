## Book an appointment

Choose the **patient**, give the appointment a **title** and optional **description**, and set when it's **scheduled**. If it follows an accepted estimate, link that estimate to it. Only accepted estimates are offered.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Booked, not started. |
| **In progress** | The patient is being seen. |
| **Completed** | Done. The completion time is recorded. |
| **Canceled** | It won't go ahead. |

Moving an appointment out of **Completed** clears its completion time.

## Invoice from an appointment

Once an appointment is **Completed**, create its invoice from it. If the appointment is linked to an estimate, the invoice starts with that estimate's line items and discount. See [Estimates and invoices](/products/dental/estimates-and-invoices/).

## Recall visits

[Recurring plans](/products/dental/recurring-plans/) can create the next appointment for you when a patient is due.
