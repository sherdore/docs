## Estimates

An estimate lists the treatment you propose. Add line items, which can come from your [treatment list](/products/dental/treatments/), and set a discount and tax rate. Each line is a **labor**, **material** or **service** item.

An estimate starts as a **Draft**. Choosing **send** marks it **Sent** and makes its link work. The link opens a page with no login. SherDore Dental doesn't email the link, so send it to the patient yourself.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Not sent yet. |
| **Sent** | The link is live. |
| **Viewed** | The patient opened it. |
| **Accepted** | The patient accepted it. |
| **Rejected** | The patient declined it. |
| **Expired** | It passed its expiry date without an answer. |

The patient chooses **accept** or **reject** and types their name as a signature. The answer is recorded and can't be changed afterwards.

## Invoices

Create an invoice from a completed [appointment](/products/dental/appointments/), or start one directly. An invoice has the same line items, discount and tax rate as an estimate.

Its status is **Draft**, **Sent**, **Viewed**, **Partially paid**, **Paid**, **Overdue** or **Void**. Marking an invoice **send** works like an estimate: it makes the patient's link live, and you share it yourself.

## Payments

Record payments against an invoice by **cash**, **check**, **card**, **bank transfer** or **other**. A payment can't exceed the remaining balance, and a part payment leaves the invoice **Partially paid**.

## Who can manage them

- **Estimates:** owners, office managers and dentists.
- **Invoices and payments:** owners, office managers and billing staff.
