SherDore Dental is practice management software for dental clinics. It keeps patient records, appointments, treatment plans, estimates, invoices and recall visits in one place.

Open it at [dental.sherdore.com](https://dental.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## How the work flows

1. A **lead** enquires. See [Leads](/products/dental/leads/).
2. You create a **patient** record. See [Patients](/products/dental/patients/).
3. You send an **estimate** for treatment, built from your treatment list. The patient accepts it from a link. See [Estimates and invoices](/products/dental/estimates-and-invoices/).
4. An accepted estimate becomes an **appointment**. See [Appointments](/products/dental/appointments/).
5. Once the appointment is completed, it becomes an **invoice**.
6. A **recurring plan** keeps routine visits, like hygiene check-ups, coming back on schedule. See [Recurring plans](/products/dental/recurring-plans/).
