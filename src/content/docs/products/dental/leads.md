A lead is a possible new patient who hasn't booked yet.

## What you record

Name, company, phone, email, location, the **source**, the **service requested**, an **estimated value**, who it's assigned to, a **follow-up date** and notes.

## Lead status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Just came in. |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out. |
| **Qualified** | It's a real prospect. |
| **Estimate requested** | They've asked for a price. |
| **Estimate sent** | You've sent one. |
| **Won** | They became a patient. |
| **Lost** | They went elsewhere or dropped out. |

When a lead is won, create their [patient](/products/dental/patients/) record.

## Who can manage leads

Owners, office managers, dentists and hygienists.
