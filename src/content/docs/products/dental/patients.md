## What a patient record holds

- **name**, **email** and **phone**
- **date of birth**
- **allergies**
- **medical notes**
- **insurance provider** and **member ID**
- a preferred way to be contacted: **phone**, **email** or **text**

A patient is either **Active** or **Inactive**. Mark someone inactive instead of deleting them so their history stays intact.

Appointments, estimates and invoices attach to the patient, so everything about a person's care is one click away from their record.

## Who can manage patients

Owners, office managers, dentists and hygienists. Billing staff can't open patient records. See [Team and roles](/products/dental/team-and-roles/).
