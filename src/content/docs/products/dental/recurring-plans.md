A recurring plan records a repeat visit for a patient, such as a six-monthly check-up.

## Create a plan

Choose the patient and set:

- a **name** and **description**
- the **frequency**: monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual
- a **price** (optional)
- a **start date**, and an **end date** if it has one

The plan tracks when the **next visit is due**, starting from the start date, and shows whether it's currently **due**.

## Schedule the next visit

When a visit is due, choose **schedule visit** on the plan. It creates the [appointment](/products/dental/appointments/) for you and opens it so you can set the time.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The plan is running. |
| **Canceled** | It was stopped. |
| **Expired** | It passed its end date. |

## Who can manage plans

Owners, office managers, dentists and hygienists.
