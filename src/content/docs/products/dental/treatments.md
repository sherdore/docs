The treatment list is your price list. Build it once and pick from it when you write estimates and invoices.

## What a treatment holds

- a **name** and **description**
- a **CDT code** (optional)
- a **default price**
- a **default duration** in minutes
- whether it's **active**

Only active treatments are offered when you build an estimate or invoice. Switch a treatment off instead of deleting it when you stop offering it.

## Who can manage treatments

Owners, office managers and dentists.
