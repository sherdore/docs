## Customers

Each customer has contact details and a preferred way to be reached: **phone**, **email** or **text**. A customer is **Active** or **Inactive**. Mark someone inactive instead of deleting them so their history stays intact.

## Tags

Tags are labels you create once and attach to leads and customers, so you can group and find them. Manage them from **Tags**.

## Custom fields

Custom fields let you record information specific to your business. Create a field definition for **leads** or for **customers**, then it appears on every record of that kind.

Choose the field's type:

| Type | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Text** | Free text, like a referral name. |
| **Number** | A quantity or amount. |
| **Date** | A date, such as a renewal. |
| **Select** | One choice from a list you define. |

Manage fields from **Custom fields**.
