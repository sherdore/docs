An email sequence is a series of steps that run on their own for each lead you enroll.

## Steps

A sequence is built from two kinds of step, in order:

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Wait** | Pauses for a number of **hours** or **days**. |
| **Send email** | Sends an email with the subject and body you write. |

For example: send a welcome email, wait 2 days, send a follow-up, wait 5 days, send a last reminder.

## Triggers

Choose how leads join the sequence:

- **Lead created**: every new lead is enrolled automatically.
- **Manual**: you enroll leads yourself from the lead page.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being built. Nothing is sent. |
| **Active** | Running. Enrolled leads move through the steps. |
| **Paused** | Stopped for now. You can activate it again. |

Use **activate** and **pause** on the sequence to switch between them.

## Tracking

Each lead's enrollment is tracked as **Pending**, **Completed** or **Failed**. The **Email Sequence** and **Automation Stats** [reports](/products/erp/reports/) show how a sequence is performing.
