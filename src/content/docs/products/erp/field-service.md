The field service module is included with your plan. It covers the work that happens after a lead becomes a customer, and it follows the same flow as [SherDore HVAC](/products/hvac/).

## Properties, equipment and technicians

A customer can have several **properties**, and each property has the **equipment** installed there. **Technicians** are the people you assign jobs to. See [Customers, properties and equipment](/products/hvac/customers-properties-equipment/) for the equipment types you can record.

## Estimates

Build an estimate, mark it as sent and share its link with the customer. They can accept or reject it online by typing their name as a signature. Estimates have both a list and a **board**, so you can see them by status: draft, sent, viewed, accepted, rejected and expired. Details are the same as [HVAC estimates](/products/hvac/estimates/).

## Jobs

From an accepted estimate, create a job, assign a technician and schedule it. Jobs have a list and a **calendar** view. Statuses are new, in progress, completed and canceled. See [Jobs and scheduling](/products/hvac/jobs-and-scheduling/).

## Invoices and payments

Create an invoice from a completed job, send it to the customer by sharing its link, and record payments by cash, check, card, bank transfer or other. You can void an invoice that hasn't been paid in full. See [Invoices and payments](/products/hvac/invoices-and-payments/).

## Recurring plans

A recurring plan books repeat service on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual cycle. Choose **schedule visit** on a plan when a visit is due to create the job. See [Maintenance plans](/products/hvac/maintenance-plans/).
