SherDore ERP is a shared operations platform. At its core is a **CRM** for leads, customers, automated follow-up and reporting. On top of that sit **industry modules**, and your plan decides which ones your organization can use. Today the module is **field service** (properties, equipment, technicians, estimates, jobs, invoices and recurring service).

Open it at [erp.sherdore.com](https://erp.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## What's in the core

- [Leads and pipeline](/products/erp/leads-and-pipeline/): a board to move enquiries from new to won.
- [Customers, tags and custom fields](/products/erp/customers-and-custom-fields/).
- [Email sequences](/products/erp/email-sequences/): automatic follow-up emails.
- [Lead capture forms](/products/erp/lead-capture-forms/): forms you share or embed to collect leads.
- [Reports](/products/erp/reports/): twelve reports, which can be emailed on a schedule.

## Modules

- [Field service](/products/erp/field-service/): property management, jobs, estimates, invoices and recurring plans.
