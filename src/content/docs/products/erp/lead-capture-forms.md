A lead capture form is a public page where anyone can send you an enquiry. No login is needed to fill it in, and each submission becomes a lead in your [pipeline](/products/erp/leads-and-pipeline/).

## Build a form

Every form asks for a **name** and an **email**. The name is required and the email is optional. You choose which extra fields to include:

- **Phone**
- **Company**
- **What do you need help with?**
- **Anything else we should know?**

You can also set:

- a **default source**, recorded on every lead from this form so you know where it came from
- a **thank-you message** shown after submitting
- a **redirect URL** to send people to your own page instead
- whether the form is **active**

## Share it

Each form gets its own web address under `/f/`, based on its name. Link to it from your website or share it directly.

## Measure it

The **Form Analytics** [report](/products/erp/reports/) shows how many people see and submit each form.
