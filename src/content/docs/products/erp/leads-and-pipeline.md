A lead is a possible customer who hasn't bought yet. Leads can be entered by hand, or arrive automatically from a [lead capture form](/products/erp/lead-capture-forms/).

## The board

The **Leads** board shows every lead in a column for its status. Move a lead to the next column as the conversation progresses.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Just came in. |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out. |
| **Qualified** | It's a real prospect. |
| **Estimate requested** | They've asked for a price. |
| **Estimate sent** | You've sent one. |
| **Won** | They became a customer. |
| **Lost** | They went elsewhere or dropped out. |

There is also a list view with search.

## Convert a lead to a customer

Choose **convert** on a lead to create a customer from its name, email and phone. The lead is marked **Won** and linked to the new customer. A lead can only be converted once. After converting, add a property for the customer to continue with field service work.

## Automate follow-up

You can **enroll** a lead in an [email sequence](/products/erp/email-sequences/) from the lead itself.

## Tags and custom fields

Group leads with tags and record extra information in your own fields. See [Customers, tags and custom fields](/products/erp/customers-and-custom-fields/).
