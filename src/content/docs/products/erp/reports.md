Open **Reports** and choose a date range. Reports are grouped by what they answer.

## Pipeline

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Lead Volume** | How many leads arrived over time. |
| **Source Performance** | Which sources bring the most leads. |
| **Pipeline Funnel** | How many leads reach each stage. |
| **Pipeline Bottlenecks** | Where leads get stuck. |
| **Source Attribution** | Which source the wins came from. |
| **Response Time** | How quickly leads get a first response. |

## People

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Agent Performance** | How each team member handles their leads. |
| **Revenue by Rep** | Revenue attributed to each person. |

## Automation

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Automation Stats** | How your automations are performing overall. |
| **Email Sequence** | Results for each email sequence. |
| **Form Analytics** | Views and submissions for each lead capture form. |

## Admin

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Audit Log** | A record of changes made in your organization. |

## Email a report on a schedule

Open **Scheduled reports** to have a report emailed automatically. Choose the report, how often (**daily**, **weekly** or **monthly**) and the email addresses to send it to. You can edit or switch off a schedule at any time.
