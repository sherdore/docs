## Customers

Each customer has contact details and a preferred way to be reached: **phone**, **email** or **text**. A customer is either **Active** or **Inactive**. Mark someone inactive instead of deleting them, so their history stays intact.

## Properties

A customer can have several **properties**, such as a home and a rental. Jobs, equipment and maintenance plans attach to a property, so you always know which address the work is for.

## Equipment

Record each unit installed at a property so its history stays with it. Choose the equipment type:

- Air conditioner, heat pump, mini split
- Furnace, boiler, water heater
- Package unit, rooftop unit, air handler
- Evaporator coil, condenser, ventilation
- Other

For heating equipment you can also note the **fuel type**: electric, natural gas, propane, oil or dual.

When you create a job you can link it to the equipment being worked on.

## Attachments

You can attach files, such as photos and documents, to records. Open an attachment from the record it belongs to.

## Who can manage equipment

Everyone except the **Accountant** role can create and edit equipment. See [Team and roles](/products/hvac/team-and-roles/).
