## Build an estimate

An estimate is a list of line items for a customer. Each line has a type:

- **Labor**
- **Material**
- **Equipment**
- **Service**

Your default tax rate, terms and expiry period (from [settings](/products/hvac/settings-and-reminders/)) are filled in for you. You can download any estimate as a **PDF**.

## Share it

An estimate starts as a **Draft**, which the customer can't see. Choosing **send** marks it as **Sent** and makes its customer link work. The link opens a page showing the estimate with no login needed.

SherDore HVAC doesn't email the link for you, so copy the link and send it to the customer yourself, by email or text. The status then tracks what happens:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Not sent yet. The customer can't open it. |
| **Sent** | The link is live. |
| **Viewed** | The customer opened it. |
| **Accepted** | The customer accepted it. |
| **Rejected** | The customer declined it. |
| **Expired** | It passed its expiry date without an answer. |

## The customer's side

On the estimate page the customer chooses **accept** or **reject** and types their name as a signature. We record the name and the time. Once they've answered, the answer can't be changed, and an expired estimate can't be accepted.

## Turn it into a job

From an **accepted** estimate, create a job. The job takes the customer, the property and the title from the estimate, and is linked back to it. You then add the technician and the schedule. See [Jobs and scheduling](/products/hvac/jobs-and-scheduling/).

Only an accepted estimate can become a job.

## Who can manage estimates

Everyone except the **Accountant** role.
