This takes about fifteen minutes and leaves you with a real customer, a job on the schedule and an invoice ready to send.

## 1. Set your defaults

Open **Organization settings** (owners and office managers can). Set the things you'd otherwise retype on every document:

- your **logo** and **brand color**, used on estimates and invoices
- the **default tax rate**
- **default terms**, printed on documents
- how many days an estimate stays valid (the **estimate expiry**)
- the **reorder threshold** for parts

See [Settings and reminders](/products/hvac/settings-and-reminders/) for every option.

## 2. Add your team

Invite people from **Team** and give each a role. See [Team and roles](/products/hvac/team-and-roles/). Then add the people who go out on jobs as **technicians**, so you can assign work to them.

## 3. Add a customer, property and equipment

Create the customer, add the property where you'll work, and record the equipment there. See [Customers, properties and equipment](/products/hvac/customers-properties-equipment/).

## 4. Quote, schedule, invoice

1. Create an **estimate** for the customer and send it.
2. When they accept, create a **job** from it and assign a technician.
3. Check the **Schedule**, do the work, and create the **invoice** from the job.

You can also create a job or invoice directly, without an estimate.
