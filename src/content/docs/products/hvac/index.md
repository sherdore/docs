SherDore HVAC is field service software for HVAC businesses. It follows the work from the first enquiry to the paid invoice: customers and their equipment, jobs and scheduling, estimates the customer can approve online, invoices, maintenance plans and parts stock.

Open it at [hvac.sherdore.com](https://hvac.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## How the work flows

1. A **lead** comes in and is qualified. See [Leads](/products/hvac/leads/).
2. You record the **customer**, their **property** and the **equipment** installed there. See [Customers, properties and equipment](/products/hvac/customers-properties-equipment/).
3. You send an **estimate**. The customer opens it from a link, then accepts or declines it with their name as a signature. See [Estimates](/products/hvac/estimates/).
4. An accepted estimate becomes a **job**, which you assign to a technician and put on the schedule. See [Jobs and scheduling](/products/hvac/jobs-and-scheduling/).
5. When the work is done, the job becomes an **invoice**. See [Invoices and payments](/products/hvac/invoices-and-payments/).
6. For repeat customers, a **maintenance plan** schedules the recurring visits. See [Maintenance plans](/products/hvac/maintenance-plans/).
