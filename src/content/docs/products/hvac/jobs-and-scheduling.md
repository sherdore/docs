## Create a job

A job is one piece of work at a property. Give it a **title** and a **description**, and choose:

- the **property** where the work happens
- the **technician** doing it
- the **equipment** it concerns (optional)
- when it's **scheduled** (optional)

You can also create a job from an accepted estimate, which carries over the customer, property and title. See [Estimates](/products/hvac/estimates/).

## Job status

A job moves through four statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Created, not started. |
| **In progress** | Work has started. |
| **Completed** | Work is done. |
| **Canceled** | The job won't go ahead. |

## Parts used on a job

Record the parts used on a job as you go. Each one is deducted from your stock and shows in the part's movement history. See [Parts and stock](/products/hvac/parts-and-stock/).

## Invoice from a job

Once a job is **Completed**, create its invoice straight from the job. See [Invoices and payments](/products/hvac/invoices-and-payments/).

## The schedule

**Schedule** lists every job that has a scheduled time, in date order, with the customer, property and technician. Filter it by technician to see one person's day. A job with no scheduled time doesn't appear until you give it one.

## Technicians

Technicians are the people you assign jobs to. Owners, office managers and dispatchers add and edit them from **Technicians**.
