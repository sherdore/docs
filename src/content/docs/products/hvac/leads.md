A lead is a potential customer who hasn't booked yet. Tracking them stops enquiries getting lost.

## What you record

- **name** and **company**
- **phone** and **email**
- **city**, **state** and **postal code**
- the **source**, such as a referral or a website form
- the **service requested**
- an **estimated value**
- who it's **assigned to**
- a **follow-up date** and **notes**

## Lead status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Just came in. |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out. |
| **Qualified** | It's a real prospect. |
| **Estimate requested** | They've asked for a price. |
| **Estimate sent** | You've sent one. |
| **Won** | They became a customer. |
| **Lost** | They went elsewhere or dropped out. |

Move a lead along as the conversation progresses. When one is won, add them as a [customer](/products/hvac/customers-properties-equipment/).

## Who can manage leads

Owners, office managers and dispatchers.
