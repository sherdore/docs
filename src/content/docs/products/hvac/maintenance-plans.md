A maintenance plan records a recurring service agreement with a customer, such as a seasonal tune-up.

## Create a plan

Choose the customer and property, then set:

- a **title** and **description**
- the **visit frequency**: monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual
- the **price** (optional)
- a **start date**, and an **end date** if the agreement has one

## Plan status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The agreement is running. |
| **Canceled** | It was stopped. |
| **Expired** | It passed its end date. |

## Upcoming visits

Upcoming maintenance visits appear in the [daily digest](/products/hvac/settings-and-reminders/#daily-digest), so your office team sees what's coming without opening the app.

## Who can manage plans

Everyone except the **Accountant** role.
