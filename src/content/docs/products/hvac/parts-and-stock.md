## The parts catalog

Add each part you stock with:

- a **name**, optional **SKU** and **description**
- its **unit** (each, foot, box and so on)
- your **unit cost** and the **unit price** you charge
- a **reorder threshold**, the stock level at which you want to reorder

New parts start with your organization's default threshold, which you can change per part. See [Settings and reminders](/products/hvac/settings-and-reminders/). Switch a part to inactive when you stop using it, rather than deleting it.

## Stock movements

Stock changes are recorded as movements, so you can see how a quantity got to where it is. There are three kinds:

| Movement | When |
|---|---|
| **Received** | Stock arrives from a supplier. |
| **Used** | Parts are used on a job. |
| **Adjustment** | You correct the count, for example after a stocktake. |

Recording parts used on a [job](/products/hvac/jobs-and-scheduling/) creates a **Used** movement and lowers the stock for you.

## Who can manage stock

Owners, office managers and dispatchers.
