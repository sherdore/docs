Owners and office managers can change these under **Organization settings**.

## Defaults

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Default tax rate** | Pre-filled on new estimates and invoices. A percentage from 0 to 100. |
| **Default terms** | Pre-filled terms printed on documents. |
| **Estimate expiry** | How many days a new estimate stays valid (1 to 365). |
| **Reorder threshold** | The stock level pre-filled when you add a new part. |

## Branding

Upload your **logo** and choose a **brand color** (a hex value such as `#4f46e5`). Both appear on your PDF estimates and invoices.

## Emails HVAC sends

### Overdue invoice reminders

Every morning HVAC emails customers a reminder for each invoice that is overdue. The same invoice isn't reminded again until your **reminder interval** has passed, which is 7 days unless you set a different number.

### Daily digest

Every morning HVAC emails your office team a digest of:

- overdue invoices
- upcoming maintenance visits

If there's nothing to report, no email is sent. By default the digest goes to the **Owner** and **Office manager** roles. You can choose which roles receive it in your settings.
