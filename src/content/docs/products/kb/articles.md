## Create an article

Open **Articles** and create one. You set:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Required, up to 200 characters. |
| **Address (slug)** | The last part of the article's web address. Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Left empty, it's made from the title. |
| **Excerpt** | A short summary, up to 300 characters. |
| **Body** | The article, written in Markdown. |
| **Category** | Where it belongs. See [Categories](/products/kb/categories/). |
| **Status** | **Draft** or **Published**. |
| **Visibility** | **Public** or **Internal**. |
| **Featured** | Highlight the article on your help center. |

Use **Preview** to see how the Markdown will look before you publish.

## Draft and published

A **draft** is private to your team. Only **published** articles appear in your help center.

## Public and internal

An **internal** article is for your team only. Customers never see it, and it's left out of search and your sitemap. Use it for support runbooks and notes you don't want public.

## Markdown, safely

The editor uses Markdown. Raw HTML is stripped and unsafe links are refused, so what you write can't break or harm your help center.

## Plan limit

Your plan caps the number of articles (250 on Starter). When you reach it, creating another asks you to upgrade.
