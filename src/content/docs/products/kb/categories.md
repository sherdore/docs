Categories group your articles on the help center, so customers can browse as well as search.

## What a category has

- a **name** (up to 100 characters)
- an **address (slug)**, made from the name if you leave it empty
- a short **description** (up to 300 characters)
- an **icon**, a short emoji or symbol
- **visibility**: **Public** or **Internal**

An **internal** category is for your team only, and customers never see it.

## Using them

Choose a category when you write an article. An article with no category still works.
