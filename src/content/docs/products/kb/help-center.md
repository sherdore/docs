Your help center is the public site for your articles. Open **Help center** to set it up.

## Your address

Your help center lives at:

```
https://kb.sherdore.com/h/your-name
```

Choose `your-name` yourself: 3 to 40 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Names are unique, and a few are reserved by SherDore.

## Make it yours

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The name shown at the top of the site. |
| **Headline** | The big line on the front page. |
| **Subline** | A short line under the headline. |
| **Accent color** | The brand color, as a hex value like `#4f46e5`. |
| **Footer** | Text at the bottom of every page. |
| **Support link and label** | A link customers can follow when they can't find an answer, such as a web page or `mailto:help@yourcompany.com`, and the words shown for it. |

## Switch it on or off

The help center has an on/off switch. While it's off, visitors see "not found".

## What customers get

- the front page with your featured articles
- article pages and category pages
- **search**
- an automatic **sitemap** for search engines

Customers read it without logging in. Articles and categories marked **internal** never appear.
