SherDore Knowledge Base gives your team a Markdown editor, categories, search and a public help center for your product, with article history and team roles.

Open it at [kb.sherdore.com](https://kb.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## How it works

1. [Write articles](/products/kb/articles/) in the editor and sort them into [categories](/products/kb/categories/).
2. Style your [help center](/products/kb/help-center/) with your name, colors and a support link.
3. Publish. Your customers read it at `kb.sherdore.com/h/your-name`, with search and no login.
