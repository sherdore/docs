Upload pictures while you write and add them to your articles. They're stored by Knowledge Base and shown on your help center.

## Storage limit

Your plan sets how much picture storage you have: 1 GB on Starter, 10 GB on Pro and 100 GB on Business. An upload that would go over the limit is refused, and you're asked to upgrade or remove older pictures.
