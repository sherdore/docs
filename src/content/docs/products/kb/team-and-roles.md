Your team writes and manages the help center together. People join through your SherDore account, and **Team** lists everyone and lets you invite more by email.

| Role | For |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The person who set the team up. |
| **Admin** | People who help manage articles and settings. |
| **Read only** | People who just need to see the content. |

Your plan sets how many people you can have: 5 on Starter, 25 on Pro and 100 on Business.

The first time someone opens Knowledge Base, their account role decides where they start. See [Your team](/getting-started/team-and-organizations/).
