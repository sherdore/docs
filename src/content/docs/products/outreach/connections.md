Outreach doesn't send from its own servers. It sends through accounts **you** own, so your messages come from your own domain and number and the costs stay with your providers.

Open **Connections** to set up each channel.

| Channel | You connect |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Your SMTP server. |
| **SMS** | Your Twilio account. |
| **WhatsApp** | Your WhatsApp Business API account. |

## Set one up

1. Open **Connections** and choose the channel.
2. Enter the details from your provider and save.
3. Choose **Test** to send a test message and confirm it works.

Secret values you enter, such as passwords and tokens, are stored encrypted and aren't shown again after saving.

## Prospecting connections

Searching for new contacts uses your own **Hunter** or **Apollo** account, connected separately under **B2B connections**. See [Find prospects](/products/outreach/prospecting/).
