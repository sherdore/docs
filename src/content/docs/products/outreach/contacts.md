## Contacts

Add contacts one at a time, or **import** a file of many. Each contact can have an email address, a phone number and extra information of your own.

## Lists

Group contacts into **lists**, such as "Newsletter" or "Customers". Campaigns and automations send to a list.

## Custom attributes

Add your own fields to contacts under **Contact attributes**, and use them to personalize messages. A field is **Text**, **Number** or **Date**.

## Consent

Every contact has a consent status, either **opted in** or **opted out**. A contact who has opted out isn't sent campaigns. Always make sure people have agreed to hear from you before you import them.

## Suppressions

Addresses that must never be sent to are kept on a suppression list. See [Deliverability and suppressions](/products/outreach/deliverability/).

## Templates

Save reusable message designs under **Templates** and pick one when you build a campaign.
