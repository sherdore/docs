The **Inbox** collects replies from all three channels, grouped into **conversations** so you can see the whole thread with a contact.

## Conversations

A conversation is **Open** or **Closed**. Close a conversation when you've dealt with it, so your open list shows only what still needs an answer.

Each message in a conversation is marked as **inbound** (from the contact) or **outbound** (from you), and shows its status: **received**, **sent**, **delivered**, **read** or **failed**.

## Getting replies in

Inbound SMS and WhatsApp replies reach Outreach through a webhook that you configure with your provider (Twilio or Meta). Until that's set up, replies arrive at your provider but not in the inbox.
