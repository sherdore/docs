**B2B search** finds business contacts for you to reach out to. It uses **your own** Hunter or Apollo account, so connect one first under **B2B connections** and choose **Test** to confirm it works.

## Search

Filter by any of:

- job titles
- locations
- company domains
- seniority
- company size
- keywords

## Reveal and import

1. Run a search and pick the people you want.
2. **Reveal** their contact details. This is when your provider counts the usage.
3. **Import** them into a [list](/products/outreach/contacts/). Each needs a name, and can carry an email, phone, job title and company.

Imported contacts then work like any others, so check consent and make sure you have a lawful basis to contact them before you send.
