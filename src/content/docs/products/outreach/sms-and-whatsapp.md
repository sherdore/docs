SMS and WhatsApp campaigns work like [email campaigns](/products/outreach/email-campaigns/), with their own sections: **SMS campaigns** and **WhatsApp campaigns**. Connect your Twilio and WhatsApp Business accounts first. See [Connect your sending accounts](/products/outreach/connections/).

## SMS delivery

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Not sent yet. |
| **Sent** | Handed to Twilio. |
| **Delivered** | Reached the phone. |
| **Undelivered** | It couldn't reach the phone. |
| **Failed** | Sending failed. |

## WhatsApp delivery

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Not sent yet. |
| **Sent** | Handed to WhatsApp. |
| **Delivered** | Reached the phone. |
| **Read** | The recipient read it. |
| **Failed** | Sending failed. |

## Replies

Replies to your SMS and WhatsApp messages arrive in the [inbox](/products/outreach/inbox/).

## Consent

Only message people who have agreed to hear from you. Contacts who have opted out aren't sent campaigns. See [Contacts, lists and consent](/products/outreach/contacts/).
