SherDore SEO fetches your pages and actually parses them, from a single URL to your whole site, then scores what it finds and tells you exactly what to fix. It also finds keyword ideas and helps you plan the content that fills the gaps.

Open it at [seo.sherdore.com](https://seo.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## The toolkit

- [Free SEO check](/products/seo/free-check/): a scored report for one URL, with no signup.
- [Reports](/products/seo/reports/): saved single-page reports for your websites.
- [Site Audit](/products/seo/site-audit/): crawl a whole site and list every issue.
- [Keyword research](/products/seo/keywords/): ideas from live autocomplete, grouped into a content plan.
- [Speed Test](/products/seo/speed-test/): Lighthouse scores and Core Web Vitals.
- [Scheduled scans, white-label and API](/products/seo/plans-and-features/) on higher plans.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't show **search volume, cost per click or difficulty**. Keyword ideas come from live Google and Bing autocomplete, and those figures need a paid data source that isn't connected.
- It doesn't **track your rankings**. It audits your pages and plans your content.
