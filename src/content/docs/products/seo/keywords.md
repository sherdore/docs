Keyword research has three tools under **Keywords**.

## Overview

Search a topic and see keyword ideas. They come from live **Google and Bing autocomplete**, and each gets an **intent label**, so you can tell what a searcher is trying to do.

## Magic tool

Start from a seed keyword and let the tool expand it into many related ideas. Export the results when you want to work on them elsewhere.

## Strategies

A **strategy** turns keyword ideas into a content plan:

1. Add the keywords you want to work on.
2. Group them into **clusters**.
3. Set priorities and track each page from **planned** to **published**.

Clusters are grouped by the words keywords share and by search intent, not by overlap in search results. Treat them as a strong starting point and move keywords between clusters by hand. You can export a strategy.

## Limits

Your plan sets your monthly keyword searches and how many strategies you can keep. See the [plans](/products/seo/#plans).

## What's not included

Search volume, cost per click and difficulty are not shown.
