## Scheduled scans

Switch scheduled scans on for a website and SEO re-scans it on a schedule, so problems show up before they cost you traffic.

## White-label

Add your brand name and logo under **Settings**, and the reports you share carry your branding.

## Team

Invite colleagues from **Team**. They join your workspace and see the same websites and reports.

## Upgrading

Plans and billing are managed in your SherDore account. The **Plans** page in SEO links you straight there.
