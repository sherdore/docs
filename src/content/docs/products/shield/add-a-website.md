## Add the site

1. Open Shield and go to **Websites**.
2. Choose to add a website, then enter its name and domain and pick its **platform**: WordPress, Magento, Laravel, PHP, Node.js, Python or static.

Your plan sets how many sites you can add.

## Verify that you own it

Before Shield scans a site, you prove it's yours. Pick one method:

| Method | What you do |
|---|---|
| **HTML file** | Upload a small file to the site with the name and contents Shield gives you. |
| **DNS TXT record** | Add a TXT record with the value Shield gives you to the domain's DNS. |
| **Meta tag** | Add a meta tag Shield gives you to your home page's `<head>`. |

Once the check passes, the site is verified and monitoring starts. You can leave the verification file, record or tag in place.

## Site status

A site is **Active**, **Paused** or **Archived**. Pause a site to stop monitoring it for a while without losing its history, and archive one you no longer need.

## Check now

Open a site and choose **Check now** to run its checks straight away instead of waiting for the next one.
