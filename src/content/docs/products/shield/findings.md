Shield keeps a separate list for each kind of finding: **Malware**, **Vulnerabilities**, **File integrity** and **Security Check**.

## Status

Every finding is in one of three states:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Needs attention. |
| **Resolved** | It was fixed. |
| **Dismissed** | It doesn't apply to you. |

Dismiss a finding that isn't relevant, for example a known and accepted setting, so it stops counting against the site. Shield tells you what's wrong and how serious it is, but fixing it is up to you or your developer.

## Severity

Malware and vulnerability findings carry a severity of **critical**, **high**, **medium** or **low**. Vulnerabilities can also be rated **none**.

## Vulnerabilities in detail

A vulnerability finding says whether it affects the **core**, a **plugin or theme**, or something unclassified, and includes fix guidance. Matches are marked **exact** when Shield is sure the version is affected, and **heuristic** when it's a likely match that you should confirm.
