SherDore Shield monitors your websites for downtime, malware, vulnerabilities and risky configuration. It scores each site and alerts you the moment something needs attention.

Open it at [shield.sherdore.com](https://shield.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## What it does

| Feature | What it gives you | Needs the agent? |
|---|---|:-:|
| **Uptime monitoring** | A check every 5 minutes, recording the HTTP status and response time. | No |
| **SSL and domain expiry** | A warning before a certificate or domain lapses. | No |
| **Site Scan** | 35 outside-in checks for exposed files, information leaks and weak configuration. | No |
| **Vulnerability detection** | Your CMS, plugins and themes checked against known vulnerabilities. | No |
| **Malware scanning** | Every file scanned for injected code, backdoors and known-bad signatures. | Yes |
| **File integrity monitoring** | Files added, changed or removed between scans are flagged. | Yes |
| **Security Check** | An audit of your PHP configuration and hardening. | Yes |
| **Backups** | Back up a site's files and database and download them. | Yes |
| **Alerts** | Email or webhook notification of the events you choose. | No |

The agent is one PHP file you add to your site. See [Deep scan agent](/products/shield/deep-scan-agent/).

## What Shield isn't

- It **finds and reports** problems with a severity and guidance. It doesn't fix them for you.
- It is **not a firewall**. It monitors and scans your sites, and doesn't sit in front of them or block traffic.

## Platforms

WordPress, Magento, Laravel, plain PHP, Node.js, Python and static sites. Each platform gets its own Site Scan checks on top of the universal ones.
