Everyone on your team works from the same dashboard. Your websites and findings belong to your team and are invisible to other teams.

## Roles

| Role | For |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The person who set the team up. |
| **Admin** | People who help manage sites and settings. |
| **Team member** | People who work with the sites day to day. |
| **Read only** | People who just need to see the results. |

## Invite someone

Open **Team**, enter their email address and pick a role. They get an invitation by email.

The first time someone opens Shield from your SherDore account, their account role decides where they start. See [Your team](/getting-started/team-and-organizations/).
