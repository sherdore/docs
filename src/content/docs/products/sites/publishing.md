Nothing is public until you publish it.

## What visitors see

A visitor to `yoursite.sites.sherdore.com` sees your website only when **both** are true:

- the **website** is published, and
- the **page** they asked for is published.

Anything else shows a "not found" page. That lets you work on new pages privately while the rest of the site stays live.

## Publish a page

Build the page, then choose **Publish**. You can keep editing afterwards and save your changes.

## No login for visitors

Published sites are public and visitors don't sign in.

## Who can publish

Publishing needs a role with publishing permission. Owners and admins have it. If a teammate can't publish, ask an owner to check their role. See [Team and roles](/products/sites/team-and-roles/).
