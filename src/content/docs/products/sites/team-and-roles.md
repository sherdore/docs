People join your organization through your SherDore account. See [Your team](/getting-started/team-and-organizations/). Inside Sites, each person has one of four roles.

| Role | For |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The person who set the organization up. Can do everything, including deleting a website. |
| **Admin** | Can manage websites, publish, and delete a website. |
| **Editor** | Builds and edits pages. |
| **Viewer** | Can look but not change anything. |

Deleting a whole website is limited to owners and admins.

The first time someone opens Sites, their account role chooses where they start.
