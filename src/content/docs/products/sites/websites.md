## Create one

Open **Dashboard** and add a website. Give it a name. Sites gives it a **subdomain** automatically, and it is always reachable at:

```
yoursite.sites.sherdore.com
```

A few subdomains are reserved by SherDore (`www`, `mail`, `webmail`, `admin`, `api`, `app`) and can't be used.

## Your homepage

Every website starts with a **homepage** already created. The homepage can be edited but can't be deleted.

## Draft and published

A website, and each of its pages, is either a **draft** or **published**. Visitors only see a website that is published, and only its published pages. See [Publish](/products/sites/publishing/).

## How many websites

Your plan sets how many websites you can have. Pro allows up to 5.
