While you write a post, describe what it's about in a sentence or two (up to 500 characters) and Social drafts a caption for you. Edit it before you schedule.

Each caption counts toward your plan's AI captions for the month: 50 on Starter, 500 on Growth, unlimited on Agency. If you've used them all, you're asked to upgrade.

AI captions are drafts. Always read and adjust them before publishing.
