## Supported platforms

| Platform | Connects |
|---|---|
| **Facebook** | A Facebook Page you manage. |
| **Instagram** | An Instagram account. |
| **X** | An X account. |

## Connect one

1. Open **Connections**.
2. Choose the platform. You're taken to that platform to approve access.
3. Approve, and you come back to Social with the account connected.

Your plan sets how many accounts you can connect. See the [plans](/products/social/#plans).

Social only posts when you tell it to, on the accounts you choose for each post.
