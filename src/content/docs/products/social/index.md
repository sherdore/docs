SherDore Social lets you write a post once, choose where it goes, and schedule it. It supports **Facebook Pages**, **Instagram** and **X**.

Open it at [social.sherdore.com](https://social.sherdore.com), signed in with your SherDore account.

## What's in it

- [Connect your accounts](/products/social/connect-accounts/)
- [Create and schedule posts](/products/social/posts/)
- [Media library](/products/social/media/)
- [Calendar and analytics](/products/social/calendar-and-analytics/)
- [AI captions](/products/social/ai-captions/)
- [Team](/products/social/team/)
