## Write a post

1. Open **Posts** and create a new post.
2. Write the **content**, up to 5,000 characters. Each platform has its own limits, so check that the text suits where it's going.
3. Choose one or more of your **connected accounts**. At least one is needed.
4. Attach media from your [media library](/products/social/media/) if you want to.

A post starts as a **draft**. You can edit a draft as often as you like.

## Schedule it

Choose a date and time and **schedule** the post. Scheduling counts toward your plan's scheduled posts for the month. You can **unschedule** a post to take it back to a draft, and delete one you don't need.

## Where to see them

The **Posts** list shows all your posts, and the [calendar](/products/social/calendar-and-analytics/) shows the scheduled ones by date.
