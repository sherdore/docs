Open **Team** to add people to your workspace. Give each person a name, an email address and a password. They can then sign in and work on the same posts and accounts.

Your plan sets how many team members you can have: 2 on Starter, 8 on Growth, 30 on Agency. Remove someone from the same page when they no longer need access.
