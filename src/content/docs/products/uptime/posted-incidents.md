Uptime opens incidents on its own when a check fails. A **posted incident** is different: it's a message **you** write for your customers, shown on your [status page](/products/uptime/status-pages/), explaining what's wrong and what you're doing about it.

## Post one

1. Open Uptime and create a posted incident.
2. Write a **title** and a **message**.
3. Choose the **sites affected**.
4. Set the **impact**: **none**, **minor**, **major** or **critical**.
5. Set the **progress**: **investigating**, **identified**, **monitoring** or **resolved**.

## Keep customers updated

Add an update as things change, with a new message and progress. Each update is kept in a timeline on the incident, with who wrote it. When it's fixed, set the progress to **Resolved**.

## Automatic and posted incidents

| | Opened by | Seen by |
|---|---|---|
| **Incident** | Uptime, automatically, when a site is down or a certificate or domain is about to expire. | Your team. |
| **Posted incident** | You, by hand. | Your customers, on the status page. |
