A status page shows your customers the current state of your sites without giving them access to your dashboard.

## What it shows

Each site appears with one of four statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Operational** | Checks are passing. |
| **Degraded** | The site is responding but not healthy. |
| **Down** | Checks are failing. |
| **Scheduled maintenance** | A maintenance window is active. |

## Set one up

1. Open Uptime and go to **Status pages**.
2. Choose a **title** and the **address** the page will live at.
3. Pick which sites to show.
4. Switch the page **on**.

You can switch the page off again at any time. Everything else about your account stays private. Only the status page you choose to publish is visible to the public.

## Visitors can subscribe

Visitors to your status page can subscribe to updates, so they hear about incidents without checking back.
