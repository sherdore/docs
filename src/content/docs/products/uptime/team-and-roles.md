Everyone on your team sees the same sites, incidents and status page.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including the team itself. |
| **Admin** | Manage sites, incidents and settings. |
| **Read-only** | View the dashboard. |

## Invite someone

Open **Team**, enter their email and pick a role. They get an invitation by email.

## How roles start

The first time someone opens Uptime from your SherDore account, their account role sets where they start: an account **owner** becomes Owner, an **admin** becomes Admin, and a **member** becomes Read-only. You can change roles inside Uptime afterwards, and those changes stick.

## Team size

Your plan limits the number of team members: 5 on Starter, 15 on Pro, unlimited on Business. When you reach the limit, inviting more people asks you to upgrade.
